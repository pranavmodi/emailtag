ERROR PATTERN ANALYSIS: Medical Records & Images → Accounts Receivable
Analyzed up to 5 examples

ANALYSIS:
• Common patterns in these misclassifications:
 – Many emails include mixed language by combining “medical records” or “images” with clearly financial or billing-related phrases such as “Billing Report Request,” “PI Billing Coordinator,” or explicit instructions to contact a billing department or payment processing service.
 – Some subjects or content mention “Medical Records & Billing Report Request” or include imaging portal language right next to billing portal language. This blend causes ambiguity when the current rules give too much weight to imaging keywords even when key billing indicators are present.
 – The “fax received” emails (even if they have an “EXTERNAL” subject) are very transactional; however, when other emails contain “Medical Records” along with “Billing” or “Lien” details and a legal context, they are being misclassified to Medical Records & Images despite the primary business function being Accounts Receivable.
 – In essence, when both imaging and billing keywords are present the decision logic has not consistently prioritized the strong financial or lien-related keywords.
