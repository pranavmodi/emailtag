Classification Rules for Medical Records & Images:

1. Subject Line Keywords:
 a. If the subject contains one or more imaging-specific keywords such as:
  – “Imaging request”, “Images”, “Radiology film”, “MRI”, “CT”, “X‑ray”, “DICOM”, “CD Request”, or related phrases,
  AND DOES NOT contain any strong financial keywords (e.g., “bill”, “billing”, “payment”, “balance”, “lien”, “W‑9”, “offer”, “remittance”, “negotiation”) OR any phrases that imply a financial transaction (e.g., “Billing Report Request”, “PI Billing”, “contact Cedar Financial”),
  then tag the email as a candidate for Medical Records & Images.
 b. If both imaging and one or more financial keywords appear in the subject, perform further review:
  – Examine the overall content for primary intent. If the email’s dominant discussion centers exclusively on managing imaging data (e.g., links or instructions to view/download images, descriptions of imaging modalities, imaging portal instructions) with minimal, incidental reference to billing, classify as Medical Records & Images.
  – If the email includes detailed payment instructions, lien processing, or scheduling of billing transactions, override imaging cues and do not classify as Medical Records & Images.
 c. If the email subject contains "A fax has arrived from" it CANNOT be Medical Records & Images

2. Content Screening:
 a. Classify as Medical Records & Images only if the body of the email includes imaging-specific phrases such as “images have been uploaded”, “view/download imaging reports”, “burn images onto a CD”, “push images into portal”, “radiology film request”, or similar language, and no paragraphs contain explicit financial directions.
 b. If any content section includes strong financial or billing phrases (e.g., “bill balance”, “process payment”, “generate bill”, “e‑sign liens”, “payment due”, “contact billing”), do not classify as Medical Records & Images.

3. Associated Tags & Metadata:
 a. Apply the Medical Records & Images category if associated tags are solely imaging related (e.g., “Image Request”, “Report Request”) and there is no accompanying billing or lien-related tag.
 b. If tags include “Bill or Balance Request”, “Bill Offer”, “Rad Lien”, “Tech Lien”, “ASL”, “SLA Applies”, or similar, immediately classify under Accounts Receivable.

4. Purpose and Context:
 a. Use Medical Records & Images only when the primary purpose is to request, share, or discuss medical imaging data exclusively.
 b. Disregard imaging references if they are provided only as supplemental information in an email whose main focus is on financial processing or lien management.
