### What's Working

1. Professional site design — dark aesthetic, clean layout, modern feel
2. Case studies have real, specific numbers (dollar amounts, hours saved, percentage improvements)
3. Blog posts demonstrate strategic thinking (AI evals as competitive moats, AI-as-user economics)
4. Domain focus on healthcare + legal is specific enough to be credible
5. Methodology is solid (workflow-first, guardrails-first, measure outcomes)
6. Key differentiator messaging is right: "production-grade engineering with domain-specific automation"

### Critical Gaps

1. **Zero search presence** — Searching for "possible minds AI" returns an unrelated academic org (possibleminds.org). No third-party mentions, no social proof trail.

2. **No human presence** — No team page, no founder story, no faces, no LinkedIn presence tied to the brand. In regulated industries, buyers need to trust a person, not just a site.

3. **Anonymous case studies** — "A 200-person California medical imaging chain" reads as unverifiable marketing copy. Named case studies with client quotes are 5x more credible.

4. **Content cadence signals side project** — Two blog posts in 18 months. In the AI space, if you're not publishing regularly, you don't exist in the conversation.

5. **No middle-ground engagement** — "Book a demo" is high-commitment. No newsletter, no downloadable resource, no free assessment, no community presence for low-commitment engagement.

6. **Missing compliance/security markers** — No mention of SOC 2, HIPAA BAA, security practices, or compliance certifications. For healthcare buyers, this is table stakes.

## Information Needed for GTM Strategy

Before building a specific playbook, these questions need answers:

### Founder & Team
- What's Pranav's background? Previous roles, companies, domain expertise?
- Team size and composition (engineering, sales, design, domain)?

### Business Fundamentals
- Is Precise Imaging the only client?
- Are the case study numbers from real production deployments?
- Can either client be named publicly (or give permission + a quote)?

### Sales History
- How was Precise Imaging acquired as a client? (warm intro, cold outreach, existing relationship?)

### Target Buyer
- Who specifically? (Imaging center ops managers? Healthcare CIOs? Law firm managing partners? RCM directors?)

- Geographic focus or national?

### Competitive Landscape Awareness
- Who are prospects comparing against? (Thoughtful AI, Infinitus, general AI agencies?)
- What objections come up in conversations?

### Capacity
- Hours/week available for business development and content?
- Ability to attend conferences, do in-person meetings?
- Budget for marketing/ads if any?

### What's Been Tried
- Cold outreach attempts and results?
- LinkedIn posting history and engagement?
- Conference attendance?
- Any partnerships or channel relationships?

## Preliminary GTM Recommendations (Pending Above Answers)

### Immediate Actions (This Week)

1. **Add a founder/team page** with real photo, background story, and why you're building this. One paragraph of authentic founder story beats an entire features page.

2. **Push to name at least one case study client.** Offer them something in return (free month, additional feature). A named client with a quote transforms credibility overnight.

3. **Start publishing weekly on LinkedIn** — not just linking to blog posts, but short-form insights about AI in healthcare operations. Consistent presence > occasional brilliance.

### Short-Term Actions (This Month)

4. **Write 2-3 deep blog posts** targeting specific buyer pain points:
   - "The True Cost of Prior Authorization for Imaging Centers" (attracts search traffic from people with the problem)
   - "How AI Agents Handle PHI: A Compliance-First Approach" (addresses the #1 objection)
   - "What Imaging Centers Get Wrong About AI Email Automation" (positions expertise)

5. **Create a free assessment or audit offer** — "We'll analyze your email volume and estimate automation potential. Free, no commitment." This is a lower-barrier entry point than "book a demo."

6. **Get into healthcare IT communities** — HIMSS, RBMA (Radiology Business Management Association), healthcare ops Slack groups, LinkedIn groups for imaging center administrators.

### Medium-Term Actions (Next 3 Months)

7. **Pursue speaking opportunities** — RBMA conferences, healthcare AI meetups, webinars. One speaking slot at an industry event is worth more than 50 cold emails.

8. **Build a referral channel** — Approach RadFlow, FrontApp, or other vendors in the imaging center stack about partnership/referral arrangements.

9. **Content marketing engine** — Weekly LinkedIn posts + biweekly blog + monthly case study update. Compound presence.

10. **Consider a pilot program** — "First 3 imaging centers get 60-day free pilot" reduces risk for buyers and generates more case studies.
