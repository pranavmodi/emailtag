Email Processing
Master switch to control all incoming email processing

🔵 When Email Processing is ENABLED:

  • Incoming emails are received via the webhook and saved to the database
  • Emails are automatically queued for AI classification and tagging
  • OCR processing is performed on attachments when needed
  • Tags are applied to conversations in FrontApp
  • Inbox routing occurs based on applied tags (if enabled)
  • All email processing workflows operate normally

🔴 When Email Processing is DISABLED:

  • Incoming emails are still received via the webhook
  • Emails are saved to the database with status "processing_disabled"
  • NO classification or tagging occurs
  • NO OCR processing is performed
  • NO Celery tasks are queued
  • Emails remain in their original state in FrontApp
  • This is useful for maintenance, troubleshooting, or preventing automated processing
