Sub-Classification Rules for Accounts Receivable Category
=========================================================
**Sub-Categories:** Billing, Liens, Negotiations, Case Updates, Collections, Process CC.

Please use the following rules to sub-classify the provided email content, already identified as 'Accounts Receivable', into one of the above sub-categories.

--- START Accounts Receivable Sub-Classification RULES ---

1. **Primary Financial Topic**: Based on the email content, determine the primary financial topic discussed and classify as follows:
   - If the email primarily concerns **invoices, payments due, account balances, W-9 requests, or general billing inquiries**, classify as 'Billing'.
   - If the email primarily discusses **legal claims on property to secure debt payment (e.g., medical liens, attorney liens)**, including requests, submissions, or modifications of liens, classify as 'Liens'.
   - If the email is primarily about **discussing, proposing, or agreeing to changes in payment terms, settlement amounts, or bill reductions**, classify as 'Negotiations'.
   - If the email primarily provides **updates on the status of a financial case, account history, or payment tracking**, including **check status inquiries, payment confirmations, attorney changes, drop letters, settlement updates, liability clearance updates, payment promises, refund requests, or payment dispatch notifications**, classify as 'Case Updates'.
   - If the email primarily relates to **overdue accounts, warnings about collections, or communication regarding accounts sent to or being considered for third-party collection agencies**, classify as 'Collections'.
   - If the email contains **explicit instructions to charge or process a credit card payment** (e.g., "please charge the credit card", "process CC payment", "run the credit card"), classify as 'Process CC'.

2. **Document Type Priority Rule**: If the email contains attachments processed by the document analysis system, prioritize classification based on document type:
   - **CRITICAL**: If ANY attachment is classified as document type **'lien_reduction'**, classify as 'Negotiations' regardless of other content, as these are settlement/reduction requests requiring negotiation. Do NOT classify as 'Liens'.
   - If ANY attachment is classified as document type **'lien'** (PSL/ASL) without lien_reduction, and the email is about submitting or requesting liens, classify as 'Liens'.

3. **Lien vs Negotiations Distinction**: 
   - **Lien reduction, settlement, or bill reduction requests** = 'Negotiations' (even if they mention liens)
   - **Lien submissions, missing lien requests, or lien modifications** = 'Liens'

4. **Case Updates vs Billing Distinction**:
   - **Check status inquiries** (e.g., "will you cash the check", "check confirmation", "voiding/reissuing checks") = 'Case Updates'
   - **Payment status updates** (e.g., "payment sent but not received", "payment on the way") = 'Case Updates' 
   - **Attorney/legal case communications** (e.g., "new attorney", "drop letter", "case pending") = 'Case Updates'
   - **Settlement status updates** (e.g., "waiting on settlement check", "liability clearance") = 'Case Updates'
   - **General billing inquiries** (e.g., "what do I owe", "invoice request", "account balance") = 'Billing'

--- END Accounts Receivable Sub-Classification RULES ---
