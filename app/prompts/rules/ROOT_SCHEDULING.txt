Sub-Classification Rules for Scheduling Category
================================================
**Sub-Categories:** RX, Cancelled, Reschedule Request, Schedule Request.

Please use the following rules to sub-classify the provided email content, already identified as 'Scheduling', into one of the above sub-categories.

--- START Scheduling Sub-Classification RULES ---

**PRIORITY RULE - ATTACHMENT-BASED CLASSIFICATION:**
⚠️ **CRITICAL**: If there is ANY attachment with document type "rx" (prescription/referral), ALWAYS classify as 'RX' regardless of email content. This takes absolute priority over all other rules below.

1. **Primary Scheduling Intent**: Based on the email content, determine the primary intent related to scheduling and classify as follows:
   - If the email primarily concerns **checking status of patient's appointment, prescriptions (RX), medication, referrals related to prescriptions, or pharmacy communications**, classify as 'RX'.
   - If the email contains **scheduling status inquiries** (e.g., "has the client been scheduled", "appointment confirmation", "scheduling updates"), classify as 'RX'.
   - If the email clearly indicates that an **appointment is no longer happening or has been voided**, classify as 'Cancelled'.
   - If the email expresses a **request to change the date or time of an existing appointment**, classify as 'Reschedule Request'.
   - If the email is a **request to make a new appointment or schedule a procedure** (with or without specific study details like MRI, CT, X-ray, etc.), classify as 'Schedule Request'.


--- END Scheduling Sub-Classification RULES ---
