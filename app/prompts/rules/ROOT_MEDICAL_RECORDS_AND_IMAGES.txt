Sub-Classification Rules for Medical Records & Images Category
============================================================
**Sub-Categories:** Release of Records, Image Request, CD Requests, Reports Request, US Tech Sheet, Portal Requests, Referral pad request, Incorrect patient info, Incorrect study info, Patient Demo, ID Mismatch.

Please use the following rules to sub-classify the provided email content, already identified as 'Medical Records & Images', into one of the above sub-categories.

--- START Medical Records & Images Sub-Classification RULES ---

1. **Specific Action or Item**: Based on the email content, determine the specific action being requested or the item being discussed related to medical records and images, and classify as follows:

   **PRIORITY RULE - Patient Demo Attachments:** If the email has an attachment classified as document type "patient_demo" (patient demographic/information forms with barcodes containing numeric DocTypeID like "019"), classify as 'Patient Demo'. These are typically patient information forms, demographic documents, or intake forms submitted alongside or separately from lien documents. NOTE: Emails with BOTH patient demo AND lien attachments should be tagged with BOTH 'Patient Demo' AND the appropriate lien tag - this is handled automatically by the system.

   **PRIORITY RULE - Mixed Requests:** If the email requests BOTH medical records/reports AND billing information (e.g., "bills & MRI records", "send bills and medical records", "bills & reports"), ALWAYS classify as 'Release of Records' regardless of other content patterns.

   **PRIORITY RULE - Portal Access/Permission Requests:** If the email requests portal access, portal permissions, portal login credentials, or ability to VIEW images/records through the portal (e.g., "give this account permission to view", "portal access for", "add user to portal", "grant access to view images"), ALWAYS classify as 'Portal Requests'. This takes priority over 'Image Request' classification.

   **PRIORITY RULE - ID Mismatch:** If the email is a notification or alert that an uploaded photo ID does not match or may not match the patient profile details (e.g., "photo ID has been uploaded that contains information that may not match the details in the patient profile", "ID does not match patient profile", "uploaded ID mismatch", "photo ID mismatch", "identity does not match"), ALWAYS classify as 'ID Mismatch'. This takes priority over 'Incorrect patient info'. NOTE: 'ID Mismatch' is specifically for photo ID verification mismatches (uploaded ID vs patient profile). Manual corrections to patient demographics (e.g., "please update the patient name", "wrong DOB") should still be classified as 'Incorrect patient info'.

   - If the email pertains to **patient portal access, permissions, or credentials** (e.g., login issues, requests for portal access, permission to view images/records through portal, adding users to portal, granting access rights), classify as 'Portal Requests'.
   - If the email involves an **authorization to share patient records or a direct request to release/send patient records**, classify as 'Release of Records'.
   - If the email **explicitly asks for medical images to be SENT/DELIVERED** (e.g., "send me the MRI images", "email the CT scans", "forward the X-Ray files/DICOMs") WITHOUT requesting billing information, classify as 'Image Request'. NOTE: Requests for permission to VIEW images on portal = Portal Requests, not Image Request.
   - If the email specifically requests that **images or reports be provided on a compact disc (CD)**, classify as 'CD Requests'.
   - If the email is a request for **medical reports** (e.g., radiology reports, diagnostic summaries, interpretations) WITHOUT requesting billing information, classify as 'Reports Request'.
   - If the email relates to an **Ultrasound Technologist worksheet, form, or similar document**, classify as 'US Tech Sheet'.
   - If the email is a request from a doctor's office or facility for **physical referral pads**, classify as 'Referral pad request'.
   - If the email highlights **errors in patient demographic information or identification details** (e.g., misspelled name, wrong DOB), classify as 'Incorrect patient info'.
   - If the email points out **discrepancies, errors, or issues with the medical study performed or documented** (e.g., wrong body part imaged, incorrect study type listed), classify as 'Incorrect study info'.
   - If the email has an **attachment that is a patient demographic form, patient information form, or intake document** (typically identified by barcodes with numeric DocTypeID like "019", or document content showing "PATIENT INFORMATION", demographic fields, emergency contacts), classify as 'Patient Demo'. NOTE: This is distinct from 'Incorrect patient info' which is for ERRORS in existing patient data. 'Patient Demo' is for SUBMITTED demographic documents/forms.
   - If the email is a **notification that an uploaded photo ID does not match or may not match the patient profile** (e.g., "photo ID has been uploaded that contains information that may not match", "ID mismatch", "uploaded ID does not match patient details"), classify as 'ID Mismatch'. NOTE: This is distinct from 'Incorrect patient info' which is for manual requests to correct patient data. 'ID Mismatch' is for automated or manual alerts about photo ID verification failures against the patient profile.

--- END Medical Records & Images Sub-Classification RULES ---
