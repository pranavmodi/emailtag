Sub-Classification Rules for Accounts Receivable - Case Updates Category
===============================================================
**Sub-Categories:** New Attorney, Drop Letter, Drop Pending, Promise to send payment, Waiting to clear liability, Waiting on settlement check, Sent payment but not received, Refund Request, Payment on way.

Please use the following rules to sub-classify the provided email content, already identified as 'Accounts Receivable - Case Updates', into one of the above sub-categories.

--- START Case Updates Sub-Classification RULES ---

1. **Nature of the Update**: Based on the email content, determine the specific nature of the case update and classify as follows:
   - If the email indicates the involvement of a **new legal representative** (e.g., new attorney, law firm change), classify as 'New Attorney'.
   - If the email is a **formal notification to drop a case or patient**, or a request to do so, classify as 'Drop Letter'.
   - If the email discusses that a **case or patient drop has been initiated but is not yet finalized** or is pending confirmation, classify as 'Drop Pending'.
   - If the email states a **commitment or promise to send a payment soon**, classify as 'Promise to send payment'.
   - If the email mentions that payment is **pending the resolution of liability issues** (e.g., waiting for insurance to determine fault), classify as 'Waiting to clear liability'.
   - If the email indicates an **anticipation of receiving payment from a settlement**, classify as 'Waiting on settlement check'.
   - If the email states that **payment was sent but has not yet been received or confirmed** by the recipient, classify as 'Sent payment but not received'.
   - If the email is a **request for money to be returned** (e.g., overpayment, incorrect charge), classify as 'Refund Request'.
   - If the email confirms that a **payment is currently en route or has just been dispatched**, classify as 'Payment on way'.

--- END Case Updates Sub-Classification RULES ---
