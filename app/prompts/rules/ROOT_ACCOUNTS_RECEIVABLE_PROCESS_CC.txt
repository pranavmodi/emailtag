Classification Rules for Accounts Receivable - Process CC Category
===========================================================

**Final Tag:** Process CC

Please use the following rules to classify the provided email content into the 'Process CC' category when it has already been identified as 'Accounts Receivable'.

--- START Process CC Classification RULES ---

**Process CC Classification Rule:**

Classify as 'Process CC' when the email contains explicit instructions to charge or process a credit card payment. This includes:

1. **Direct Credit Card Processing Instructions:**
   - "Please charge the credit card"
   - "Process the credit card payment"
   - "Run the credit card"
   - "Charge the card on file"
   - "Process CC payment"
   - "Execute credit card transaction"

2. **Credit Card Authorization Requests:**
   - "You are authorized to charge my credit card"
   - "Please proceed with charging the card"
   - "Go ahead and process the payment via credit card"
   - "Charge my card for the balance"

3. **Payment Method Specifications:**
   - "Use the credit card for payment"
   - "Pay via credit card"
   - "Charge to my Visa/MasterCard/etc."
   - "Process payment using the card ending in [numbers]"

**Important Distinctions:**
- This is for IMMEDIATE processing instructions, not general billing inquiries
- Different from "Bill or balance Request" which asks for billing information
- Different from payment promises or settlement discussions
- Must contain explicit instruction to charge/process a credit card

**Key Phrases to Look For:**
- "charge", "process", "run" + "credit card"/"CC"/"card"
- "authorized to charge"
- "proceed with payment"
- Specific card details with processing instructions

--- END Process CC Classification RULES ---
