Sub-Classification Rules for Scheduling - RX Category
=====================================================
**Sub-Categories:** Order Pending, Order Entered, Incomplete, RX, Apt Status Req, No Show, Cash Patient.

Please use the following rules to sub-classify the provided email content, already identified as 'Scheduling - RX', into one of the above sub-categories.

--- START RX Sub-Classification RULES ---

1. **RX-Related Status or Request**: Based on the email content, determine the specific status or request concerning a prescription (RX) or related appointment, and classify as follows:
   - If the email indicates that a **prescription order is awaiting processing, confirmation, or further action**, classify as 'Order Pending'.
   - If the email confirms that a **prescription order has been successfully entered into the system**, classify as 'Order Entered'.
   - If the email states that **necessary information is missing to process a prescription or referral**, classify as 'Incomplete'.
   - If the email has an attachment of type RX and/or is a **direct request for a new prescription or a refill**, classify as 'RX'.
   - If the email is an **inquiry about the status of an appointment, scheduling confirmation, or asking if a patient has been scheduled** (e.g., "Has the client been scheduled?", "What's the appointment status?", "Is the patient scheduled for imaging?"), classify as 'Apt Status Req'.
   - If the email reports that a **patient did not attend an appointment associated with a prescription or referral**, classify as 'No Show'.
   - If the email mentions that the **patient will be paying cash for services related to a prescription or referral**, classify as 'Cash Patient'.

--- END RX Sub-Classification RULES ---
