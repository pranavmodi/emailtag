Rules for classifying items under Negotiations into one of its children: Bill Offer, Pending Approval, Offer Approved, Counter Offer Sent.

--- START Negotiations Sub-Classification RULES ---

1. For emails concerning Negotiations, identify the negotiation stage:
   - If an initial offer on a bill is being made, consider 'Bill Offer'.
   - If an offer or proposal is awaiting a decision, consider 'Pending Approval'.
   - If an offer has been accepted, consider 'Offer Approved'.
   - If a revised offer has been sent back in response to a previous offer, consider 'Counter Offer Sent'.

--- END Negotiations Sub-Classification RULES ---
