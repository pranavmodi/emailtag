Rules for classifying items under RX into one of its children: Order Pending, Order Entered, Incomplete, RX Request, Apt Status Req, No Show, Cash Patient.

--- START RX Sub-Classification RULES ---

1. For emails categorized under RX, determine the specific status or request:
   - If the order is awaiting processing or confirmation, consider 'Order Pending'.
   - If the order has been successfully entered into the system, consider 'Order Entered'.
   - If information is missing for the prescription, consider 'Incomplete'.
   - If specifically asking for a prescription or refill, consider 'RX Request'.
   - If inquiring about appointment status related to a prescription, consider 'Apt Status Req'.
   - If the patient did not attend an appointment related to RX, consider 'No Show'.
   - If the patient is paying cash for prescription-related services, consider 'Cash Patient'.

--- END RX Sub-Classification RULES ---
