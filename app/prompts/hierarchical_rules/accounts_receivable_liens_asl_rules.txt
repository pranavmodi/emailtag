Rules for classifying items under ASL into one of its children: Rad Lien, Tech Lien, Lien Modified.

--- START ASL Sub-Classification RULES ---

1. For emails under ASL (Attorney Service Lien), specify the lien type:
   - If the lien is for radiology services, consider 'Rad Lien'.
   - If the lien is for technologist services, consider 'Tech Lien'.
   - If an existing ASL has been changed or updated, consider 'Lien Modified'.

--- END ASL Sub-Classification RULES ---
