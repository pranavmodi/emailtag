Rules for classifying items under Scheduling into one of its children: RX, Cancelled, Reschedule Request, Schedule Request, Will contact patient.

--- START Scheduling Sub-Classification RULES ---

1. To classify within Scheduling, analyze the primary intent:
   - If related to prescriptions, medication, or pharmacy, consider 'RX'.
   - If indicating an appointment is no longer happening, consider 'Cancelled'.
   - If requesting a change to an existing appointment, consider 'Reschedule Request'.
   - If asking to make a new appointment, consider 'Schedule Request'.
   - If stating an intention to reach out to the patient, consider 'Will contact patient'.
