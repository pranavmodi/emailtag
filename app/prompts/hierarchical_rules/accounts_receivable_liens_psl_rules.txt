Rules for classifying items under PSL into one of its children: Rad Lien, Tech Lien, Lien Modified.

--- START PSL Sub-Classification RULES ---

1. For emails under PSL (Prescription Service Lien), specify the lien type:
   - If the lien is for radiology services, consider 'Rad Lien'.
   - If the lien is for technologist services, consider 'Tech Lien'.
   - If an existing PSL has been changed or updated, consider 'Lien Modified'.

--- END PSL Sub-Classification RULES ---
