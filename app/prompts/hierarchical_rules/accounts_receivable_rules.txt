Rules for classifying items under Accounts Receivable into one of its children: Billing, Liens, Negotiations, Case Updates, Collections.

--- START Accounts Receivable Sub-Classification RULES ---

1. To classify within Accounts Receivable, identify the primary financial topic:
   - If related to invoices, payments, balances, or general billing inquiries, consider 'Billing'.
   - If discussing legal claims on property for debt (medical liens, attorney liens), consider 'Liens'.
   - If about discussing or proposing changes to payment terms or amounts, consider 'Negotiations'.
   - If providing updates on the status of a financial case or account, consider 'Case Updates'.
   - If related to overdue accounts being sent or considered for third-party collection agencies, consider 'Collections'.

--- END Accounts Receivable Sub-Classification RULES ---
