Rules for classifying items under Collections into one of its children: To send to collections, Sent to Collections, Attorney Non-Payment, To Review.

--- START Collections Sub-Classification RULES ---

1. For emails related to Collections, identify the collection status or issue:
   - If an account is being considered or prepared for a collection agency, consider 'To send to collections'.
   - If an account has already been transferred to a collection agency, consider 'Sent to Collections'.
   - If an attorney has failed to make a payment on an account, consider 'Attorney Non-Payment'.
   - If an account or situation requires a manual review for collections, consider 'To Review'.

--- END Collections Sub-Classification RULES ---
