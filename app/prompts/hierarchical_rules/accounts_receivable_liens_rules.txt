Rules for classifying items under Liens into one of its children: Missing Lien Request, PSL, ASL.

--- START Liens Sub-Classification RULES ---

1. For emails categorized under Liens, determine the type or status:
   - If a required lien document has not been received or is being requested, consider 'Missing Lien Request'.
   - If related to a Prescription Service Lien, consider 'PSL'.
   - If related to an Attorney Service Lien, consider 'ASL'.

--- END Liens Sub-Classification RULES ---
