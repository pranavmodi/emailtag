Rules for classifying items under Case Updates into one of its children: New Attorney, Drop Letter, Drop Pending, Promise to send payment, Waiting to clear liability, Waiting on settlement check, Sent payment but not received, Refund Request, Payment on way.

--- START Case Updates Sub-Classification RULES ---

1. For emails providing Case Updates, determine the nature of the update:
   - If a new legal representative is involved, consider 'New Attorney'.
   - If a formal notice to drop a case/patient is sent, consider 'Drop Letter'.
   - If a case/patient drop is initiated but not yet finalized, consider 'Drop Pending'.
   - If there is a commitment to send payment soon, consider 'Promise to send payment'.
   - If waiting for liability to be resolved before payment, consider 'Waiting to clear liability'.
   - If anticipating a payment from a settlement, consider 'Waiting on settlement check'.
   - If payment was sent but not yet received/confirmed, consider 'Sent payment but not received'.
   - If requesting money to be returned, consider 'Refund Request'.
   - If a payment is en route, consider 'Payment on way'.

--- END Case Updates Sub-Classification RULES ---
